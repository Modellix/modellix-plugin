# Palettes

Name the palette in the brief. In the prompt, describe the ground, the main color, and one accent in ordinary color words. Do not print palette names, color names, or hex codes as visible text in the image.

| Palette | Ground, main, accent |
| --- | --- |
| warm | Cream ground, terracotta and amber, a deep brown accent. Personal and close. |
| elegant | Ivory ground, charcoal and a muted gold accent. Formal and spare. |
| cool | Pale gray-blue ground, steel blue, a sharp cyan accent. Technical and calm. |
| dark | Near-black ground, a single bright accent, low fill light. Cinematic. |
| earth | Sand ground, olive and clay, a leaf-green accent. Outdoor and material. |
| vivid | A saturated ground, a second saturated color, white only for the title. Loud. |
| pastel | A light tinted ground, two softer tints, no black. Gentle. |
| mono | One hue from paper to ink. No second color family. |
| retro | Aged paper, faded red and teal, a mustard accent. Printed and slightly worn. |
| duotone | Exactly two colors. One is the ground, one is every shape and the title. |
| macaron | A pale ground and two candy tints, evenly spaced. Teaching and friendly. |

Mood changes the same palette. Subtle lowers contrast. Balanced keeps it. Bold pushes the accent and the title apart from the ground.
