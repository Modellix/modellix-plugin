# Casting board

The deliverable is one landscape image. Say the aspect as a ratio. The default is 16:9. Omit pixel sizes. The image contains no letters, numbers, logos, captions, or watermarks.

## Frame

Warm ivory ground. Even studio light. A soft shadow under the full-body figures. Two rounded cards sit on the left and the right. The center is a large portrait with no card behind it.

| Zone | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Left card | A 3×3 grid of head-and-shoulders shots. Same camera distance. Expressions and small head turns differ. Same hair, costume, and light in every cell. |
| Center | One chest-up portrait of the same person, looking slightly off the lens. This portrait is the largest element. |
| Right card | Three full-body figures standing: front, three-quarter, and back. Feet are visible. The pose is neutral and the arms are relaxed. Same costume. |

The same identity occupies every zone. Do not add a fourth person, a label under a cell, or a prop that crosses from one zone into another.
